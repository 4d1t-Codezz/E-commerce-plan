  • Kill rule: an ad set that spends 2× your target CPA (about $50) with zero purchases gets turned off. An ad with no add to carts after $25 gets turned off.
  • Scale rule: a winner (ROAS ≥ 2 for 2 days) gets +20% budget every 48 hours. Don't double overnight; it resets learning.
  • Don't touch a new ad set for the first 48 hours unless it is clearly burning money.

What we need to ask Miguel

Things that can kill the store

  • Fake reviews. The FTC banned fake and AI-written reviews in 2024, with fines per review, and Meta and Shopify Payments both ban stores for it. Instead: send free units to 5 to 10 friends or micro creators for real reviews and photos, and turn on post-purchase review emails from day one.
  • Stolen videos. Reposted creator content gets copyright strikes, and repeated strikes are a fast way to lose an ad account. Use AI-generated video (Higgsfield), your own filming, or paid UGC creators with usage rights.
  • New ad accounts. Brand-new Meta accounts that spend big on day one get restricted. That's why the warmup starts Oct 30 and ads only scale after Nov 15.
  • Shipping times. If the product takes 15+ days, Black Friday buyers won't get it before Christmas and you'll eat chargebacks. Pick a supplier with US stock or 7 to 10 day shipping, and state shipping times clearly.
  • Payouts on hold. Shopify Payments and PayPal often hold funds for new stores when sales spike. Keep ad money separate from expected revenue.
