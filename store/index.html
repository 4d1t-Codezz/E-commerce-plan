How many chews should my dog get?
Under 25 lb: half a chew. 25–60 lb: one chew. Over 60 lb: two chews. Give with food once a day. For the first two weeks you can double the dose to build up faster.

The ingredients are widely used and well tolerated, but if your dog is on prescription medication, check with your vet before starting any supplement.
